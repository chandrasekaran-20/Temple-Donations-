About the Temple

Sithambareswarar Temple is a sacred place of worship dedicated to Lord Siva. Located in the peaceful village of Mettupatti, the temple has been a spiritual center for devotees for generations, providing blessings and spiritual guidance to all who visit.

Your generous donations help us maintain the temple, conduct religious ceremonies, and support community welfare activities.
