About the Temple

Arulmigu Sivakamasundari Udanurai Chithambareswarar Temple is a sacred abode of Lord Shiva, where the divine presence of Goddess Sivakamasundari accompanies the presiding deity Chithambareswarar. The temple is a beacon of spirituality, tradition, and devotion, drawing countless devotees who seek the blessings of the Lord for peace, prosperity, and liberation.

The temple conducts daily rituals, abhishekam, special poojas during festivals such as Maha Shivaratri, Karthigai Deepam, Aadi Pooram, and Panguni Uthiram. Your donations directly fund the upkeep of the sanctum sanctorum, the conduct of yagnas, annadhanam for pilgrims, and the preservation of our ancient cultural heritage for future generations.
